__TITLE__

Discover the climate headlines for your area, based on strong forecast model agreement. Conditions are compared to what's typical at a given location for the time of year.

    These headlines provide a general overview of possible climate patterns in the coming months, compared to what's typical at each location. On the maps, the color is determined by whichever side has more models. A light color is a majority. A deep color means every model agrees. A tie has no shading. Anomalies use each model's hindcast for 1993-2016. Data is sourced from the Copernicus Climate Change Service.
